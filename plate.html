Aranea

A dusting of sand on a drumhead. Every figure the sand draws is a true mode of a circular membrane — the same shapes Chladni bowed out of glass — and every mode is a note.

The inner rings are the deep modes. Work outward and around for the high ones. One hand keeps the ground, the other wanders.

Hold a key and the sand settles into its figure. Chord two and it draws them both at once.
