Aranea

Every ring is a fretted string: the bright strands hold the key, and the dim strands between them carry every note in between — a full fretboard bent into a spiral.

Spun true, the wedges are fretted to the reaches — every step of the spider's tune lands on a strand it is already touching, so the song is a short walk rather than a hunt.

Hold a strand and it sings on. Record loops, keep the webs you love.
